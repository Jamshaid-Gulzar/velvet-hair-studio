Welcome to Velvet

Velvet Hair Studio opened in 2016. We are a small team of experienced stylists who love what we do.

We use quality vegan products and always take time to listen to what you want. Our studio is a relaxed space where you can sit back and enjoy a cup of tea while we look after your hair.

We look forward to seeing you soon.
